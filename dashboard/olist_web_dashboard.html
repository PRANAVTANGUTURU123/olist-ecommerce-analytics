Delivery & logistics

Actual vs. estimated delivery time, where delays concentrate, and the link to review scores.

Review score by delivery outcome
The single strongest driver of satisfaction in this dataset
An 8+ day late delivery costs 2.6 stars (4.32★ early vs. 1.73★ very late). Olist's delivery estimates run generous — orders land 11.2 days ahead of the promised date on average — which cushions most orders but leaves late ones exposed.
